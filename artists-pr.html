What Artists PR Looks Like in 2026

Art PR is a discipline of context — critics, gallerists and editors receive endless invitations to openings, viewings and studio visits. Standing out requires a hook beyond ‘a new show is opening’. The strongest art pitches tie the work to a wider cultural conversation, a critical debate, a market moment, or a genuinely unique artist story.

In Australia, ArtsHub, Art Collector, and Art Guide Australia are key targets for exhibition coverage and artist profiles. In the UK, The Art Newspaper, Frieze, and Apollo Magazine drive the international conversation. In the US, Artforum, ARTnews, and Hyperallergic reach the most influential critics, curators and collectors globally.

Art podcasts are one of the fastest-growing channels for reaching engaged audiences — particularly for emerging artists, mid-career profiles, and exhibition launches. Hosts who care about the work introduce artists to listeners who actively visit galleries, attend openings, and buy.

Artists PR — Common Questions

Everything you need to know about getting your art, exhibition or studio covered by press.

A strong exhibition press release leads with the news — the show title, the artist(s), the gallery, the dates — in the first paragraph. Then add a single tight paragraph on the work itself: what it explores, what's new about it, and why it matters now. Always include a quote from the artist or curator, high-resolution installation or work images, and a clear boilerplate about the gallery.
