What Music & Entertainment PR Looks Like in 2026

Music and entertainment PR is one of the most relationship-driven disciplines in media — but a well-crafted press release still opens doors, especially for new releases, tour announcements, label news, or streaming milestones. The key is understanding what different music journalists cover and tailoring your pitch to their specific beat.

In Australia, Triple J, Tone Deaf, and The Music Network are essential for music industry coverage. In the UK, NME, Music Week, and BBC Music are the key targets. In the US, Billboard, Rolling Stone, and Variety Entertainment set the agenda for the global music and entertainment industry.

Music and entertainment podcasts offer a powerful alternative to traditional press — particularly for artists and labels who want to reach passionate fans in a long-form, intimate format. These audiences are intensely loyal and convert at a higher rate than passive social media followers.
